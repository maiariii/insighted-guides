Slide 02

The Nexus & Home Tab

Centralized entry point and macro-level financial/compliance summaries.

The Nexus Launchpad: Primary hub for apps. Locate and click the SIIF Module card to initiate monitoring.
Total Recipients: Displays the count of eligible schools authorized to receive SIIF allocations.
Submitted Plans: Indicates the count of schools with finalized/submitted plans, along with the compliance rate.
Appropriation Progress Bar: Visually displays the percentage of the budget currently appropriated by schools.
ℹ️
Color Indicators

• Yellow Indicator: Total cumulative budget allocated to your office jurisdiction.
• Blue Indicator: Total budget sum currently appropriated in submitted plans.

Slide 04

Schools Tab & Advanced Filters

Primary monitoring roster equipped with status sorting and precise filters.

Roster Views: Toggle between Table format (optimal for detailed desktop audits) and Cards format (optimized for mobile).
Status Quick Filters:
  • All: Lists all recipient schools in jurisdiction.
  • Submitted: Shows schools with finalized plans.
  • Pending: Shows schools drafting their proposals.
  • Overdue: Highlights schools missing the deadline.
Funnel Advanced Filters: Click the Funnel Icon to filter by Budget Parameters, student Beneficiary Counts, and planned Intervention Types (Remediation, Enhancement, Enrichment).
⚠️
Allocation vs. Estimate

Verify estimate amounts. If a school's estimate exceeds their authorized allocation, contact the school head immediately to adjust.

Slide 05

School Details & Export Report

In-depth modal overlays for plan evaluation and structured reports downloading.

🔍
Appropriation Progress

A prominent visual bar indicates the exact percentage of the school's allocated budget utilized in their proposed plan.

📂
Intervention Navigation

Clickable internal tabs display cost summaries and grade-level beneficiary charts for each proposed intervention.

📋
Planned Activities Registry

Presents a numbered register of all operational tasks and procurements planned by the School Head.

📥

Report Exporting

Click the Download Icon located at the top-right of any school details overlay to export a formalized PDF or HTML report.
