Slide 02

The Planning Hub (Proposal Creation)

A strict, sequential 4-step lock system with deadline-driven editing gates.

⏳
Strict Deadline Enforcement

The system operates under a firm timeline prominently displayed. After the deadline passes, the Hub automatically switches to a read-only lock.

🔒
Sequential Locking System

Each step builds on the previous one. Confirming one step unlocks the next block, ensuring logical progress and data consistency.

✍️
Modify Mode

Click the "Modify Hub" button or navigate directly to the Forms tab to initialize or revise drafts before final submission.

ℹ️
Step-by-Step Flow

You must complete the Planning Hub in sequence:
Step 1: Interventions → Step 2: Beneficiaries → Step 3: Activities → Step 4: Budget Estimates.

⚠️
Submission Rule

Changes to plans cannot be submitted once the deadline passes. Review details carefully before locking each module.

Slide 03

Planning Steps 1–3: Selection & Scope

Defining what interventions your school will run and who benefits.

1
Step 1: Select Interventions Pick the specific innovation programs you want to run by checking their cards. For Remediation (ARAL), identify the target subjects. Review summaries, type CONFIRM, and save to unlock Step 2.
2
Step 2: Define Beneficiaries Input exact targeted learner counts, cleanly grouped by Key Stage (e.g., KS1: K-3, KS2: 4-6) and specific Grade Levels. Tally, type CONFIRM, and save to unlock Step 3.
3
Step 3: Map Out Activities Tick standardized choices aligned with school policies (e.g., SIP-AIP Aligned Activities, Action Research, or utilization of leftover balances). Use "Others" for custom entries. Type CONFIRM and save to proceed.
Slide 04

Step 4: Budget Estimation Guardrails

Financial safeguards directly comparing estimates to official allocation boundaries.

Real-Time Tracker: As values are written, the total plan cost updates instantly.
Allocation Guardrails: System matches overall cost against the school's official budget limit.
Submission Lock: If the budget estimate exceeds the limit, the confirm button freezes, and an Over Limit alert displays.
💡
Balancing Action

You must adjust estimates downward until the total cost fits within official limits to save or submit.

Slide 05

Submitting Your Proposal

Final validation step before formal submission to Division Office.

After ensuring all four steps in the Planning Hub are marked as confirmed with green labels:

View Summary: Tap the "View Summary" button at the bottom of the Planning Hub page.
Full Audit Review: Double-check learners count, programs selected, and budget breakdown.
Final Submit: Hit the Submit button. The plan locks permanently for division level review.
✅

Proposal Complete

Once submitted, the system logs the timestamp and locks the submission file from further local modifications.

Slide 06

Quarterly Utilization & Safeguards

Reporting quarterly expenditures with strict spending logic gates.

📅 Period Lock

Expenditure reporting is locked to specific months (e.g. July, October, January). Outside these windows, fields are strictly Read-Only.

⛔ Overspend Stop

You cannot enter an expenditure that exceeds the planned budget for any individual intervention. Input gets blocked instantly.

📈 Cumulative Lock

The system tracks spending across quarters. If an intervention allocation is fully spent, the field locks automatically.
