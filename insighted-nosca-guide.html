Slide 04

JHS Quotas & Schools

Allocate Junior High School items strictly within registered position allocations.

⚠️
Quota Limit Rules

Junior High School (JHS) items require explicit school assignments matching the nosca_school_allocation_jhs registry allocations.

🟢 Situation 1

Normal: School has remaining quota allocation. Select school name and click confirm to complete assignment.

🟡 Situation 2

Exceeded: Allocation is 0. Bypassing requires adding a Justification Remark and identifying the UACS/Original School to transfer allocation.

🔴 Situation 3

Not on Registry: School is completely absent. Justification remark and deduction from an original school is strictly required.

Slide 05

Submitting & Registering

Commit verified item batches to the master registry database.

✓
Assign All JHS Items

Unassigned Junior High School entries will block submission. Every JHS position must map to a valid school.

✓
Provide Serial Number

Input the batch's unique NOSCA Serial Number before submitting to maintain strict database indexing.

✓
Duplicate Checking

The system validates that no item number in the upload batch already exists in any division registry across the platform.

Slide 06

Master Item Ledger

Central tracking directory for all vacancy states and historical deployments.

🔍 Search & Filter

Locate specific items instantly. Filter by category, school name, item number, or deployment vacancy status.

🔄 Reassignment

Transfer deployed items between schools. The system automatically enforces Situation 1-3 quota rules during reassignments.

🛡️ Audit Logging

Historical trails are captured. Every deployment, edit, and transfer records the actor context, timestamp, and modification details.
