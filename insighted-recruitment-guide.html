NOSCA Upload & Vacancy Creation

How HRMOs ingest plantilla positions and map CSC qualification standards.

📤
HRMO NOSCA Ingestion

The HRMO uploads the NOSCA document into InsightED. The system automatically reads and extracts: the NOSCA reference, authorized position/item, office or unit assignment, salary grade (SG), and vacancy reference details.

🛡️
Central Office Qualifications (CSC Res. 26000920)

Job Descriptions (JDs) and Qualification Standards (QS) are NOT uploaded by HRMOs. Instead, they are centrally managed and automatically linked using the CSC-approved qualification requirements (Education, Training, Experience, and Eligibility).

📅
Publication Compliance

Created open positions are set to Draft, Open, or Screening. Publication periods automatically synchronize with the CS Form 9 revised 2025 submitted to the CSC.

Vacancies & QS Standards

The HRMO Dashboard interface for tracking vacancy statuses and applicant counts.

📁 Source File Ledger

HRMOs can trace back any position to its original uploaded NOSCA source file for auditing and verification purposes.

🔍 Qualification Tracker

Provides a side-by-side view of the position's Job Description, CSC qualifications, and specific required documentary attachments.

📊 Application Metrics

Real-time counts showing: active applicants, pending reviews, hiring/development purposes, and current recruitment stages.

💡
Position Status Matrix

Positions advance through state markers: Draft (initial setup) → Open (accepting applications) → Screening (HR paper review) → Shortlisted (top candidates forwarded) → Closed/Filled (hiring finalized).

PDS Profile Registration

Reusable PDS-style records that simplify applying to multiple vacancies.

📝
PDS & CS Form No. 212 Profiling

Applicants register and set up a reusable profile containing standard Personal Data Sheet (PDS) and Work Experience Sheet fields: Personal info, Contact, Education, Training hours, Work history, and Eligibility.

📁
Document Attachment Vault

Upload civil service certificates, board ratings, transcripts (TOR), and performance evaluations (IPCR) directly to the profile vault once.

🔗
Multi-Vacancy Applications

Apply to one or multiple vacancies across published batches. Each application is scored and evaluated independently against that specific position's requirements.

Pipeline & HRMO Deliberation

Filtering applicants, resolving evaluations, and sending automated scheduling alerts.

1

Paper Screening Statuses

Applicants are categorized as: Qualified (meets QS, complete documents), Disqualified (does not meet minimum QS), or Excluded (incomplete documents/missed deadline).

2

Smart Filtering & Query

Compare candidates instantly by training hours, relevant experience, board ratings, score ranges, and documentation completeness scores.

3

HRMPSB Deliberation

System displays comparative assessment results. Appointing authority approves final selection, logging overrides, reviewers, timestamps, and comments.

4

Email & In-App Alerts

Advancing applicants triggers automated email/mobile schedules for WST, WE, or BEI. Selected candidates receive congratulatory offers, while others receive regret notices.
