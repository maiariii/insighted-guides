Ingest & Open Vacancy: Steps 1 – 2

Importing scanned plantilla NOSCA documents and publishing vacancies.

📤
1. Upload the NOSCA to the System

Navigate to the Vacancies tab, then click the Add Vacancy button to open the upload modal. Upload the official scanned Plantilla or NOSCA document. The system parser automatically scans the document to extract plantilla item details, office assignments, and Salary Grades (SG) in real-time.

🔄
2. Open the Vacancy to Accept Applications

On the Vacancies ledger list, click the action toggle button in the row to change the posting status to Open for Application (turns into a green badge). This publishes the vacancy to the external applicant portal to permit submissions.

Candidate Application: Step 3

How applicants search open roles and submit verified credentials.

📝
3. Applicants Submit Applications

Candidates register through the external portal, select the target open plantilla item number, and fill out their online Personal Data Sheets (PDS) along with uploading required credentials (certificates, license cards) before the deadline.

Initial Evaluation: Steps 4 – 6

Vetting candidate parameters, setting statuses, and automatic promotion.

📋
4. Conduct the Initial Evaluation Review

Navigate to the Applications tab, select the applicant's row, and review their Education, Experience, Training, and Eligibility parameters side-by-side with Civil Service (CS) minimum standards.

⚙️
5. Mark Meets-Qualification Applicants as Qualified

Assign qualification standard checkmarks. Candidates meeting all requirements are marked as **Qualified** (green badge). Fails trigger *Disqualified*, and incomplete folders are set to *Excluded*.

⚡
6. Automatically Forward to Assessment Stage

Upon marking an applicant as Qualified, the system's pipeline engine automatically routes them to the next evaluation phase—the **Assessment Stage**—where they instantly appear on the assessment board list.

Candidate Assessment & CAR: Steps 7 – 9

Entering scores, checking ranks under Rule-of-Five, exporting files, and submitting to SDO/RO.

🪜 7. HRMO Conducts Assessment & Assigns Ratings

Navigate to the Assessment tab. Select candidate rows to input scores for exams, interviews (BEI), work simulation (WST), and IPCR performance. The system automatically computes the **Overall Scorefit** percentage.

📄 8. Generate & Download the CAR

Check the automated rankings. Verify the top 5 candidates have the green Rule of 5 badge. Click **Download CAR** in the Actions card to export the final Excel rankings sheet.

📤 9. Submit CAR to Appointing Authority

Forward the downloaded CAR Excel spreadsheet and the candidates' verified qualification portfolios to the Appointing Authority (SDO/RO chief) for executive vetting.

Selection & Appointment: Steps 10 – 11

Appointing Authority review, confirming selections, and generating appointment letters.

⚖️
10. Appointing Authority Reviews CAR & Selects Applicant

The Appointing Authority reviews the candidate profiles, overall scores, and evaluator remarks listed on the CAR report, and selects the applicant to be appointed to fill the plantilla vacancy.

⭐
11. HRMO Prepares Letter of Appointment

Navigate to the Appointment tab. Select the appointee's row and click **Confirm Appointment** to lock the candidate to the plantilla item. Download and print the generated official **Letter of Appointment**.
