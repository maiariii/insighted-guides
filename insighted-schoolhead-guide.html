InsightED CLOUD Module

InsightED Mobile App

InsightED is a school information dashboard system designed to guide schools in collecting and organizing essential data across all units.

Slide 01

School Profile

Basic identity and official parameters of the school.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Basic Identity: Record the official 6-digit School ID, the full school name, and the school head's information.
  • 2. Location Coordinates: Pin the exact location of the school using the mapping tool to establish the coordinates.
  • 3. Classification: Select the levels of education offered (such as Kinder, Elementary, or Secondary) and the school ownership (public or private status).
💡 Core System Rule: This profile serves as the foundation of the system because all other records are connected to the school profile for identification and reporting.

Slide 02

School Enrollment

Total student registries and special learning support cohorts.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Enrollment Counts: Record the total number of officially enrolled learners in the school.
  • 2. Special Cohorts: Identify special learner groups such as those in the ARAL Program (Grades 1–6) or other special programs.
  • 3. Resource Needs: Use this registry to track special learning needs, supporting planning for teachers, classrooms, and resources, and identifying learners requiring additional academic support.
💡 Update Registry: Ensure all general and special program enrollment figures are updated per school year for accurate monitoring.
Slide 03

Organized Classes

Classroom distribution and optimal section organization.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Section & Group Setup: Record how learners are grouped into sections and check the class size per section in the classroom registry.
  • 2. Resource Efficiency: Balance class sizes to optimize classroom assignments and ensure efficient use of teachers and rooms.
  • 3. Class Types: Setup multigrade combinations where sections are combined.
💡 Registry Goal: Use this data to monitor for overcrowded or underutilized classes and improve overall learning conditions.
Slide 04

Learner Profile

Detailed demographic subgroups, dropouts, and student BMI status.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Learner Subgroups: Collect information for specialized categories including ALS learners, Muslim learners, and Indigenous Peoples (IP).
  • 2. Special Contexts: Record displaced learners and overage learners in the school.
  • 3. Dropout Track: Input dropout cases from both the previous and current school years.
  • 4. BMI Status: Record nutritional status based on Body Mass Index (BMI) measurements.
Slide 05

Shifting & Modality

Schedules, shift configuration, and Emergency ADM protocols.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Shift Configuration: Check if the school follows a single-shift, full in-person setup or uses multiple shifts.
  • 2. Modality Mapping: Map schedules for each grade level.
  • 3. Emergency Setup: Track Alternative Delivery Modes (ADM) used during disasters, emergencies, or classroom availability constraints.
Slide 06

School Resources

Audit classrooms, ICT equipment, mobile eCarts, utilities, and WASH.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Audit Classrooms: Track classroom facilities by grade level.
  • 2. ICT & Mobile Tools: Record quantities of ICT equipment (such as computers and tablets) and mobile learning tools (eCarts).
  • 3. Utilities & Hygiene: Record utility services like electricity and internet, alongside WASH facilities (water, sanitation, and hygiene).
Slide 07

Physical Facilities

Record buildings, rooms, dimensions, capacities, and space maps.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Inventory Structures: Record school buildings, rooms, and physical structures.
  • 2. Dimensions & Capacity: Input building inventory details, room dimensions, usage type, and seating capacity.
  • 3. Space Map: Categorize rooms by grade level or function (instructional or non-instructional) and use the visual builder to map school spaces.
Slide 08

School Terrain

Environmental accessibility, transport, community hazards, and threat metrics.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Accessibility Parameters: Log transport access including road conditions, lighting, and public transport availability.
  • 2. Local Hazards: Identify nearby environmental hazards such as rivers, cliffs, or flood-prone areas.
  • 3. Services & Threats: Map community points like hospitals, barangay halls, and transport terminals, and log natural calamities experienced, alongside social or man-made threats.
Slide 09

Infrastructure & Safety

Electrical inspection, CCTV systems, emergency tools, and safety audits.

📋 How to Complete This Section:

  • 1. Electrical Safety: Monitor electrical systems, wiring age, and inspection records. Audit the safety of power boxes, outlets, cords, and lighting.
  • 2. Security & Safety Equipment: Log active CCTV systems, emergency tools, and disaster readiness equipment (such as fire exits and flashlights).
  • 3. Safety Item Inventory: Audit fire extinguishers, first aid kits, emergency radios, and electrical maintenance logs.
